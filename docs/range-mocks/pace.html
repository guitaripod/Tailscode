Range — the pace bar

One grammar for every tank: the solid fill is what has been used, the tick is where an even burn would be by now (how much of the window has elapsed), and the hatched tail is where the fill lands at the pace so far. A line of words appears only when the tail has something to say; a row that is on pace stays two lines, exactly as shipped.
