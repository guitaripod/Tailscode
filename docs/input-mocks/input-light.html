One button, and its glyph is the verb a press will perform: send; queue behind the running turn; stop it; save the rewrite of a waiting message; take that message back when the box was emptied. A change of verb is a change of glyph (the symbol replaces itself), the button springs as it arms on the first character, and on a send the arrow leaves upward on the same beat as the prompt rises.
