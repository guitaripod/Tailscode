# Store set for 1.84 (2026-11-21)

1.84 keeps the buyer's story of 1.83 ("Claude Code, from your phone") and its captions. What changes is the app under them: the prompt box, Home's row of buttons and folded quota strip, the first run's one question, the Live Activity drawn as a tile of the Agents wall, and the iPad's columns. Every master that shows one of those screens is shot again from the 1.84 build, in every caption language, and the panels are composed from the new masters.

## 1. The week 1.83 has been live (baseline)

From Apple's App Store Discovery and Engagement Standard and App Downloads Standard reports (daily instances processed 2026-11-14 to 2026-11-20, identical rows dropped, iPhone only, unique counts for the search card):

| iPhone | 2026-11-11 to 11-13 (before 1.83) | 2026-11-15 to 11-19 (1.83) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unique search impressions a day | 212 | 474 |
| Search impression to page view | 5.5 % | 2.5 % |
| First downloads a day | 2.3 (11-01 to 11-11, `marketing-2026-11.md`) | 2.8 |

The new name ("Tailscode: Claude Code Remote") more than doubled how often the app is shown in search, and the share of those people who open the page fell by more than half: the name reaches searches that are not looking for this app. First downloads rose a little. Five days is too short to separate the name from the pictures, and 1.83's own judgment (`marketing-2026-11.md` §6: 6 % and 4 a day) still stands.

## 2. How 1.84 is judged

A week after 1.84 is live, pull the same two reports the same way and compare with §1: iPhone unique search impressions a day, search impression to page view, and first downloads a day. 1.84 changes no words in the listing, so a move in the tap-through after 1.84 belongs to the pictures and the release, not the name. Report a miss plainly.
