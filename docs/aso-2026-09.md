# Tailscode ASO — September 2026 revision (shipped with 1.45)

Why: the September CFO review put Tailscode at the portfolio's best proceeds per
install (€0.39, $14.99 Pro, 3.6 % of installs buy) on its worst page-view
rate (1.5 % of 40,771 impressions in 1–14 September). Pulling the raw
Discovery & Engagement report by device (3–14 September) showed where that
number really comes from:

| Device | Search impressions | Product page views | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mac (Mac App Store) | 35,765 | 193 | 0.5 % |
| iPhone | 2,329 | 152 | 6.5 % |
| iPad | 1,652 | 53 | 3.2 % |

88 % of the impressions are the Mac App Store, where a Developer Tools app
surfaces for broad queries and almost nobody opens it; the iPhone funnel is
healthy but tiny. Two structural facts cap both: the app name was the bare word
"Tailscode" (the heaviest keyword field carried no keyword), and the account
held one rating. Baseline ranks (2026-09-16, `aso-rank.py`, US storefront):
`claude code` #40, `opencode` unranked, `coding agent` #93, `tailscale` #47,
`oh my pi` #43, `remote coding` #76, `vibe coding` #78, everything else
unranked; `opencode` #26 in GB/DE/JP. Sales 5–15 September: 33 iPhone and 8
Mac first-time downloads, 1 Pro unit.

## What changed

### 1. The name carries the category
`Tailscode` → **`Tailscode: Coding Agent Remote`** in every locale. The
competitors that rank for `claude code` all describe themselves in the name
(Paseo – Remote Coding Agents, Clawdex: Code from Phone, OpenCode Mobile App,
Pi Relay); Tailscode's name matched nothing. "Claude Code" stays out of the
name on purpose (5.2.1 exposure) and in the subtitle, where it has been
approved for weeks.

### 2. Subtitles, and three new locales for cross-indexing
Apple indexes secondary locales per storefront. The US indexes en-US plus
es-MX, fr-FR, pt-BR, zh-Hans, zh-Hant, ko (and ru/ar/vi, not carried); the UK
indexes en-GB + en-AU; Australia en-AU + en-GB; Germany, the Netherlands,
Sweden, India etc. index en-GB as their second set; Japan indexes en-US.

- en-US subtitle `Claude Code & opencode client`
- new **en-GB** `Drive Claude Code & opencode`, **en-AU** `Claude Code &
  opencode, live`, **es-MX** `Cliente Claude Code y opencode` — descriptions
  and promo copied from en-US / es-ES, screenshots fall back to the primary set
- the other nine subtitles are unchanged

### 3. Keyword fields re-cut as English developer terms
Every field drops the words the name and subtitle now carry (coding, agent,
remote, claude, code, opencode) and spends its 100 characters on the terms this
audience actually types: `tailscale`, `llm`, `git`, `vibe`, `homelab`, `ai`,
`developer`, `phone`, `lock screen`, `pi`, plus a few native words per locale
(`ki`, `entwickler`, `開発者`, `코딩 에이전트`…). The fr/pt/zh/ko fields matter
twice: at home and in the US index. Canonical copy: `docs/asc-listing-l10n.json`
(iPhone) and `docs/asc-listing-l10n-macos.json` (Mac), pushed by
`scripts/asc-listing.py <version>`, which now also writes the name and creates a
locale the store does not have yet.

### 4. Screenshots: recaptured, re-ordered, captioned in ten languages
`scripts/shots.sh` takes `TAILSCODE_SHOT_LOCALE=<locale>` and writes the
app's chrome in that language to `marketing/appstore/l10n/<locale>/iphone`;
`marketing/captions/<locale>.json` holds each locale's headlines;
`scripts/market-compose.py` renders them through AppKit (so kana, hangul and
han get the right system face) into `marketing/appstore/panels/l10n/<locale>`.
Panel 1 now names the product ("Claude Code, from your phone"), the approval
card moved to panel 2, and the board to 3 — the three that show in search.
The Mac's first panel names the products too.

### 5. Product: the store is one row away (1.45)
Settings ▸ About gains **Rate Tailscode** (the write-review form) and **Share
Tailscode** (the listing, with one sentence, in the share sheet) —
`AppStoreLinks`, capability `rateAndShare` in the parity registry (Mac: gap,
Linux: not applicable). The automatic prompt (`ReviewPromptPolicy`, loosened
2026-09-08) has been live since 1.42.
