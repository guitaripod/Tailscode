# Neural trace: implementation contract

The build plan for `docs/neural-trace.md`, `docs/neural-trace-states.md` and `docs/neural-trace-performance.md`. Those three are the design; this one fixes names, files, rules and gates so the pieces built in parallel fit. The reference behaviour is the browser mock: `docs/neural-trace-mocks/src/` (`engine.js` layout, edges, ties, ganglia and node cards; `hero2.js` states, the frame function, the shaders; `f.js` the conversation integration, replay, provenance and answer streaming; `data.js` the fixtures). A port starts from those files and adapts them to the real types.

## Decisions that are fixed

- One brain per turn. Everything a turn did before its final message is the brain: thinking, tool calls and the agent's earlier notes. The final answer is the trailing text after the last tool or reasoning part of that turn.
- The brain replaces the activity run rows ("N tools" lines, standalone thinking rows). The old rows stay as the **Lines** style (`ActivityStyleSetting`, key `tailscode.activityStyle`, default Brain) so a person, a GPU without ASTC and a shed budget can keep them.
- Metal on iOS and the Mac. A CPU delta compositor (pure Swift, in Core) on Linux, because an animating `GtkGLArea` leaks about 70 MB a minute on the vendor's EGL stack (`CGtkShim/shim.c`, the orb comment). The same compositor is the reference for the shader, the golden for the tests and the painter for share posters.
- 30 fps (`ActivityTuning.frameRate`, 24 minimum, pinned with `.activityTempo` / `runAtActivityTempo()`), a frame only while something moves, pause with no window, off screen, occluded, inactive. Dive and scrub may use the display's rate.
- Only one brain is open per window; textures are shared per process and live while any brain is open.
- Nothing is invented: wires are sequence, calls of one message and literal path or command ties; replay uses message times; provenance is literal.
- No code comments except `///` doc comments, no MARK, no file headers (repo rule). Strings through `Localized.text`. Swift 6 strict concurrency, Core types `Sendable` and never `@MainActor`.

## Core: `TailscodeCore/Sources/TailscodeCore/Neural/`

Design space is 640 by 420, y down. Zone centres: think (456,150), act (348,104), sense (204,162), shell (240,284), delegate (396,250), say (520,306). The usable slots of a zone are the hex-spiral positions (spacing 28, say 24; 19 candidates, say 7) whose node (radius 10) lies inside the dome outline (the jaw ellipse for say); the capacities are think 19, sense 19, act 16, shell 12, delegate 13, say 7 and a test asserts them. The dome outline is `M128,212 C112,132 176,72 268,54 C366,38 462,52 512,108 C540,142 534,196 512,234 C494,260 452,268 420,270 L334,298 C296,314 254,322 214,314 C168,302 134,266 128,212 Z` sampled in 24 steps per curve; the jaw is the ellipse centre (520,306) radii 58 and 40.

- `NeuralGlyph.swift`: the outline points, per-zone dots (radius and opacity by count), the eye bar and halo, an optional ring, for SwiftUI paths, Core Graphics and cairo at 28 to 46 pt.

- `NeuralTimeline.swift`: message times to replay time, gaps over 20 s compressed to about 1.2 s, default playback fitted to about 12 s; `NeuralProvenance.swift`: `steps(touching:in:)` literal matching on input and output and `tokens(in:)` finding path-like tokens in an answer; `NeuralShare.swift`: the poster geometry (1080 wide, 56 padding, hero 968 by 590, six zone meters, quote, footer) as `AnalyticsShare.Card` does.
- `NeuralAssets.swift`: the asset manifest (names, formats, sizes, packing of the 12 channels into 3 RGBA masks), `NeuralImage` (width, height, RGBA8), `NeuralAssetStore` (loads by name through a client-supplied `(String) -> Data?`, shared per process, `retain()`/`release()` so it is resident only while a brain is open).
- `NeuralCompositor.swift`: `NeuralCompositor`, the CPU renderer: `init(assets:size:palette:)`, `renderFull(_ frame:) -> NeuralSurface` (interior and front as BGRA premultiplied 8-bit), `renderDelta(_ frame:, dirty:)` recomputing only the active zone's rectangle, the eye region and a sparse fibre list, work split with `concurrentPerform`. It implements exactly the formulas in `hero2.js` (`FS_INT`, `FS_FRONT`): linear light, the masks times tone times intensity times 4, the bloom times 2 (a third of the baked value times 3), the screen blend of `g / (1 + 0.2 g)`, the sweep, the crunch, the rainbow, the effort segments, the ribs.
- `ActivityStyle.swift`: `ActivityStyleSetting` (brain or lines; Linux captures it in `SettingsFile`).
- Tests in `TailscodeCore/Tests/TailscodeCoreTests/Neural*Tests.swift` (Swift Testing): zone capacities, partition rules, layout against the mock's fixtures (`BUILD_FIX` and `TURN` re-expressed as `ChatMessage`s), state table, frame constants (breath 1.9 s floor 0.34, heartbeat 1.6 s floor 0.42 with beats at 0.08 and 0.25), glow lifting, timeline, provenance, share geometry, compositor invariants (a settled frame equals `renderFull`, delta equals full on the dirty region, offline is dark).
- Capability: `AppCapability.neuralTrace` with its `CapabilityDefinition` (area "transcript") and each client's manifest answer; strings into `Resources/Localizable.xcstrings` with `scripts/l10n-check.py --add FILE.json` (de, es, fr, it, ja, ko, pl, pt-BR, zh-Hans, zh-Hant).

### The turn partition (all clients use it)

A turn is the run of assistant messages between two user messages (or the end). Walk its parts in order. A `.reasoning` part with words is a thinking step. A `.tool` part is a step in the zone of its `summaryKind` (fileRead, fileSearch, webSearch, webFetch: sense; fileEdit, fileWrite: act; shell: shell; subagent, workflow, skill: delegate; taskTracking and question: think; other: sense); tool kinds that already draw a card (subagent, workflow, design board, task board, question) are steps with `isCard` true, and the client still emits their card row. A `.text` part before the last tool or reasoning part is a note (a say step). The trailing `.text` parts after the last tool or reasoning part are the answer, and are not steps. While the turn runs the trailing text is the answer in progress; if a tool or reasoning part later follows, it becomes a note. `.file`, `.compaction`, `.note` and unknown parts are never steps. `memberPartIDs` lists the parts the brain consumes (reasoning, plain tools, notes); a card tool's part is a member only for the node, its card row is still built. A tool part's status maps: pending and running to running (or waiting when an approval is pending for it, or answer for a question awaiting), completed to ok, error to failed, stopped to stopped; a running tool the machine reports stuck is stalled. The turn's outcome: running while `status == .running`; failed when its last message has an error; stopped, interrupted and answerless as the Kit reports them; otherwise settled.

## Assets

`scripts/neural-head/render.sh` renders the layers; `scripts/neural-head/pack.py` (new) turns them into `Resources/Neural/`: the packed and resized set (chassis, interior, glass at half resolution, three RGBA masks of 12 channels without the visor backing, three bloom textures at 1/8) as ASTC (chassis 4×4, the rest 6×6, with mips, in a small container: magic, format, size, mip lengths, bytes) for the Apple clients and as RGBA8 PNG for the CPU compositor and tests. iOS bundles the ASTC set; the Mac bundles it too (an Intel Mac without ASTC keeps the Lines style and the glyph); Linux installs the PNG set to `share/tailscode/neural/` (and finds `Resources/Neural/` beside a development checkout, or `TAILSCODE_NEURAL_DIR`). Core never calls `Bundle.module`.

## Renderers

- Apple (iOS and Mac), shared source in `Shared/Neural/` added to both targets in `project.yml`: `NeuralMetalRenderer` (two pipelines from `NeuralShaders.metal`, function constants for the light-theme spill, ripple, sweep, crunch and rainbow, the dome drawn as a triangle fan from `NeuralDome.triangleFan`, sRGB views for the colour layers, ASTC with mips, a three-buffer uniform ring filled from `NeuralFrame`, no per-frame allocation), a `CAMetalLayer` or `MTKView` that follows the orb's lifecycle (`isPaused` when settled, no window, off screen, occluded or inactive) at `.activityTempo`, `maximumDrawableCount = 2`. The interior is an opaque layer, the front (chassis, glass, the eye, ear and ribs glow) a transparent premultiplied one, the nodes layers between, exactly as the browser mock stacks them.
- Linux: `NeuralCompositor` into three overlaid drawing areas (interior, nodes, front); the tick at the activity tempo through the existing tick helper; a frame only when `NeuralFrame.settled` is false or the hover, theme, size or dive changes.
- Nodes (all clients): a shape per zone (circle with a dashed halo, diamond, chipped square, hexagon, octagon with an inner ring, speech bubble), numbered, tone from the status (waiting and stalled attention, failed danger, running live), the waiting node's heartbeat and the running node's breath animated by the render server on Apple (`CAKeyframeAnimation` with `runAtActivityTempo()`) and by the tick on Linux; hover or focus lights the node's wires and shows its card, a click pins it; past 60 nodes the Apple nodes are drawn as instanced SDF shapes in the same pass instead of layers.

## Chat integration (per client)

Each client has its own row builder; each emits one new row per turn, `brain:<turn id>`, at the position of the turn's first member part, and skips the member parts' own rows. The row is a collapsed line (the 28 pt glyph, the count, the mix and duration, `+N −M`, a Rows pill) or, when open, the line plus the stage (the layers and nodes), the one-line ticker, and the scrubber once settled. No turn is open until the person opens it; opening one closes the one that was open, and the choice is kept for the conversation. Rows lists the old step rows inline. The header's status line, the dock, the composer and the approval and question cards are untouched. In-conversation find searches a brain's steps; accessibility reads `NeuralReading.spoken` and exposes each step; reduced motion holds every state still.

- iOS: `ChatRowBuilder.makeRows`, `ChatRow.Content` (a new `brain` case and its arms in `makeCell`, `ChatSpacing.rowClass(of:)` and `ChatFind.searchText`), a cell that hosts the process's one brain host view only while open, expansion in `expandedReasoning`-style state keyed by row id with the scroll anchor held (`revealToggledRow`), the live row's cascade untouched.
- Mac: `TranscriptRowBuilder` (a `Kind.brain`), `RowHost` sizing, `PaneLifetime` releasing the stage when the pane parks, `ChatChecks` additions, `--tree` entries.
- Linux: `TranscriptRow.Kind.brain`, `TranscriptRow.makeWidget`, the row memo key, `TAILSCODE_DRIVE` verbs (`brain=open|close|state=<id>|theme=<id>`), `SelfTest` checks, screenshots through `scripts/dev-linuxapp.sh shot`.
  Second pass (the card, the docked stage, the readout, the leader and the move, `docs/neural-trace-readout.md`): the row is one `TailscodeBrainRow` container (`brain.c`) whose children (card surface, picture, words and buttons; the stage's interior, nodes, front, leader and hit layers; the readout, its strip and the footer; the move's canvas) are placed at the rectangles `NeuralStageMetrics` gives for the row's width at the moment the row is allocated. `BrainCard` paints a shared rest picture (`BrainPictures`, one compositor render per look and size, never per card) with its own lights and rings, and builds its words lazily; `BrainReadout` (header, scrolling body, facts, cross-faded 90 ms between two pages, diffed by subject) and `BrainFooter` draw `NeuralReadout`; `BrainLeader` draws `NeuralLeader`'s route along its length; `BrainMove` applies `NeuralTransition` on the display clock, painting one traveller (the stage's own two pictures scaled, the card's lights fading, the nodes inside its transform, the sweep as a screened gradient) while the row's height eases and the scroll anchor holds. The compositor runs off the main loop on a serial queue, latest request wins, rendered no larger than 1100 by 720 device pixels and stretched by cairo; a brain owns a clock only while its turn runs, a replay or a dive is going and it is in view; the governor's critical level has three seconds of hysteresis in and ten out and never rebuilds the stage. `TAILSCODE_DRIVE` verbs added: `brain=card|unpin|readout=digest|now|zone=<z>|transition=<0..1>[,close]|transition=release|anim[=slow]|width=<points>|text|dump|demo=cards`.

  Third pass (the step list, a smaller head, one cursor; `docs/neural-trace-readout.md` section 7): the head is the map at `NeuralStageMetrics.stage` (280 to 340 points, flush with the gutter, so the leader's canvas is placed at the head's own origin) with `BrainFilters` (one canvas: the zone panel with count bars and keys, or the chips under 900 points or with fewer than three zones) at its left and `BrainList` under it. `BrainList` is one `TailscodeListCanvas` (a drawing area announced as a list) that paints only the rows the viewport shows from `NeuralStepList.rows(in:)` (zone edge, 18 point glyph box, number, title, middle-ellipsised mono argument measured by character width, fan-out bracket drawn only between neighbours, failed wash, cursor wash with ring and bar, hover wash and Copy button, status marks), owns the wheel (a target the scroll eases toward; `scrollTo` is the same ease), the Following pill, the empty-filter state, the key-cap hint, a scroll thumb and the search field (a real `GtkEntry` placed over the header while open). Nothing in it is a widget per row: a 2,000-step turn holds 307 widgets in the whole open row (the readout's digest lists six failures and six files and says how many more), paints about 0.5 ms a frame and draws at most the rows in view. Every key and press is mapped to a `NeuralBrowse.Action` in `BrainBrowse.swift`; the model runs the reducer and the effects it returns are the only things the client performs (scroll, clipboard, focus, legend popover, row menu, ring, dim). The brain is a focus group: `BrainHost.handleKey` offers a key to a brain only while the keyboard is on its list, head or readout (`hasBrainFocus`), so the window's normal-mode chords that collide (`j k G g g [ ] n p x / o ? space`) belong to the brain there and to the window everywhere else; the search field takes text and nothing else does. Hover is a preview and is rate-limited (`routeHover`: the first at once, a burst folded into the newest, a hover of what is already shown is nothing), the cursor is the model's pin. Under 860 points the row is an accordion: the cursor's row opens in place to the readout's content inside a clip, one at a time, eased over the frames the list owns. The readout swap rule (never build into the page that is still fading, never remove a widget from inside its own callback) and the weak-widget rule (`Gtk.WeakWidget` for anything a posted hop or timer holds, popovers taken off their parent before it goes, focus moved before a focused widget is removed, `disposed` set before a row's children are unparented) hold in every part. An explicit open builds the stage at any governor level as one settled picture with no clock and no move (`stageMayOpen`); only the latest turn's automatic open, the clock and the move are shed. `BrainView.holdPictureForTest`, `BrainBudget.force`, `TAILSCODE_STRESS_FULL` and the `brain=key|click|filter|search|menu|menuitem|legend|scroll|expand|rowhover` drive verbs are the harness's hooks.

  Fourth, fifth and sixth passes (the account, the entity's window, the live card and the move both ways; `docs/neural-trace-readout.md` sections 8 to 10): the right side of the open brain is `BrainAccount` (`BrainAccount*.swift`), a virtualised story drawn from Core's `NeuralAccount`: its own scroll offset eased by `exp(-dt/0.075)`, item offsets from measured heights (estimated until an item has been made), an anchor item held across every change of height and for 0.45 s after a resize, a realisation window of the viewport plus 0.8 of it each way, deferred realisation and deferred removal (never remove a widget inside its own callback, move the focus to the story before a cell goes), the wheel taken in the bubble phase. The window is a header (who, effort, the verdict pill from `NeuralVerdict.Turn.pillWord` and its sentence), the ask pinned above, the Now line and a running turn's plan, then the story: Asked, Plan, Said notes in the answer's own role, Thought, work lines as clauses (`NeuralAccount.Work.clauses(limit: 4)`: a passed build or test wears a check, a fixed error is an amber clause with the retry arrow, an unfixed one a red clause with the cross, "+N more") and the Result. `AccountEntry` is the one place a kind of item is added: a case, a builder in `BrainAccountCells`, a line in the key. Two columns from an account width of 924 (the detail card beside the story), one column with the card in place below, and under 860 points a narrow account with the filter chips above it. `BrainRowPainter` draws a step list row and a work line's rows with the same pixels. Said is easy to find (a SAID chip first with its count and the digit 1, 40 point two-line rows, SAID solo makes the account the transcript of what it said), Reply quotes into the composer through `PromptEditor.insertReplyQuote` and sends nothing, Copy flashes Copied. A fixed error is amber everywhere it appears (`BrainDraw.verdictGlyph`: check, retry arrow, cross, triangle, dot, pause bars, dashed clock, square, so no verdict is told by colour alone), and every word, tone and mark comes from `NeuralVerdict`, `NeuralHuman`, `NeuralAccount` and `NeuralBrainCard`. Nothing opens by itself (`NeuralOpenPolicy`): the card is the resting state of every turn. A running turn's card is alive (`BrainCardLive`): the headline and the NOW line are two small canvases laid over the settled words, which wait invisibly under them, so a settle is a cross-fade and nothing moves; the head is two compositor frames (the dimmest and the brightest instant of the breath), rendered once each, mixed by `NeuralLiveCard.swell`; the tone edge has a canvas of its own so a pulse repaints three points of width; one clock serves every live card on screen (absolute time, so they breathe as one hand) and a settled card owns none; a note holds the line for four seconds, a silence of 40 s replaces the output; when the turn ends the card settles over 400 ms by `NeuralCardSettle`. The move (`BrainMove`) applies `NeuralTransition` frames from the display clock: open or close from a settled or a live card (the traveller carries `travellerSwell`, and a breathing light on the active zone keeps its beat through the move), the tone edge riding the picture's left side for 90 ms, the row clipped and its height following the same progress so the transcript below rides it, the account fading in as the NOW line leaves, reversal from the current frame, reduced motion a 120 ms crossfade and a strained window 160 ms without sweep or stagger. Measured on a release build at 1920 by 2160 (process CPU over 30 s windows, the demo app with a settled card closed is 4.6 percent of a core): a live card closed adds 3.0 to 3.4 points (30 frames a second of one 132 by 86 picture and a three-point edge, each a frame of the toolkit on the cairo renderer), a settled brain open costs nothing over the floor, a live turn open 12.0 percent and a streaming one 13.3; the resident set is flat over twenty open and close cycles (339 to 346 MB, 305 after); on a 2,000-step turn the account holds at most 182 widgets in a scroll (279 in the whole open row), 302 scrolled frames run at a median of 16.6 ms with a worst of 18.4, a solve costs 66 us and the main loop is never more than 6 ms late. The brain selftest is 394 claims in 130 s in a debug build and 85 s in release on a machine with a load average above ten; most of that is fixed waits for the stage's first picture, the settle and the sweeps, and it did not meet the 25 s of the third pass. Harness hooks: `brain=height|quote|settletest|settlearm|settlefreeze|acctscroll|acctbench|account`, `TAILSCODE_BRAIN_GROUP` (one or several selftest groups) and the `live`, `live settle`, `live moves` and `account` groups of `SelfTestBrainLive.swift` and `SelfTestBrainAccount.swift`.



## Away and sharing

- Live Activity: the head glyph (44 pt on the Lock Screen, 38 pt expanded) replaces `PhaseIcon`'s symbol; compact and minimal stay symbols. Notification long look and `NoticeFace`: the head at 34 to 46 pt in the tone tile. Both draw `NeuralGlyph`.
- Share a turn: a poster painted from `NeuralShare` with the hero from `NeuralCompositor` (iOS and Mac through their existing card renderers, Linux through `BrainShareDialog`, which paints the poster with cairo and Pango from the same geometry: the hero is the stage's own picture, the CPU compositor's full frame with `BrainPainter`'s nodes, wires and ties laid between its interior and its front).
- Haptics: node fire plays `.step`, a waiting node `.needsYou`, settling `.received`.

## Existing code to improve

The audit of the three clients (clocks that ignore settled, hidden or occluded; per-frame allocation; the orb's CPU cost; work per streamed token) is applied in the same change; its findings and what was done are listed at the end of `docs/neural-trace-performance.md`.

## Gates

Core: `cd TailscodeCore && swift test` passes (new Neural tests included), `scripts/parity.sh --check` passes, `scripts/l10n-check.py --strict` passes. Linux: `swift build` and `--selftest` pass headless, shots of every state and theme through the harness, the resident set stable over a two-minute live turn, CPU time per frame recorded. Mac: `scripts/build-mac.sh` compiles, `--selftest` and the chat checks pass, shots through the macbook. iOS: the simulator build compiles and runs the demo chat. Installs follow the repo's rules (`scripts/install-linuxapp.sh`, `scripts/install-macapp.sh`; iOS is the person's to install).
